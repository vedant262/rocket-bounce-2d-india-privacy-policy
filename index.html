1 Overview

This Privacy Policy describes how Dragoster Studios ("we", "us", or "our") handles information in connection with the Rocket Bounce 2D India mobile game application ("the App") available on Google Play.

The App is free to play and is supported by advertising. We use Google AdMob to display banner and rewarded video ads. To do this, AdMob collects and uses your device's Advertising ID for ad delivery and measurement. We do not otherwise collect personal information, and the App has no login or account system.

🛡️ Short version: No accounts, no personal info, no logins. The App shows ads through Google AdMob, which collects your device's Advertising ID to serve and measure those ads. Your game progress is saved locally on your device only.

2 Information We Do NOT Collect

Beyond the Advertising ID used for ads (see Section 3), the App does not collect any of the following:

✕ Personal information ✕ Email addresses ✕ Names or usernames ✕ Precise or approximate location ✕ Usage analytics ✕ Crash reports ✕ Purchase history ✕ Contact information ✕ Photos, camera, or microphone access
3 Advertisements

Rocket Bounce 2D India displays ads through Google AdMob (the Google Mobile Ads SDK), specifically banner ads and rewarded video ads (the latter optionally grants in-game rewards in exchange for watching an ad).

To serve and measure these ads, AdMob collects and shares your device's Advertising ID (Android Advertising ID / GAID, declared via the com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID permission). This is used for advertising and ad-performance measurement purposes only — we do not use it for anything else, and we do not otherwise sell or share your data with third parties.

You can opt out of personalized advertising at any time via your device settings: Settings → Google → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalization (wording varies by Android version). Google's own handling of this data is governed by the Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

✓ Google AdMob ✓ Banner ads ✓ Rewarded video ads ✓ Advertising ID collected & shared ✕ No other ad networks
4 Internet & Permissions

The App requires an internet connection to load and display ads. Gameplay itself runs entirely on your device, but network access is used by the AdMob SDK to request and serve ads.

The App requests only the permissions needed for this: internet access and network state (for ads), and the Advertising ID permission described above. We do not request access to your camera, microphone, contacts, storage, or location.

5 Login & Accounts

Rocket Bounce 2D India has no login system, no account creation, no password, and no user profile of any kind. You simply download and play — no sign-up, no registration, nothing required.

6 Third-Party Services

The only third-party service integrated into the App is Google AdMob, used solely to display ads as described in Section 3. We do not use Google Analytics, Firebase, Facebook SDK, crash-reporting tools, or any other third-party SDK.

✓ Google AdMob ✕ No Google Analytics ✕ No Firebase ✕ No Facebook SDK ✕ No other third-party SDKs
7 Children's Privacy

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children beyond the Advertising ID used by AdMob for ad serving, as described above. If you believe a child has provided us with information beyond what's described in this policy, please contact us using the details in Section 10 so we can address it.

Advertising within the App is served through Google AdMob in accordance with Google Play's Families Policy and applicable regulations such as COPPA. If our audience settings in Google Play classify this App as directed at children, ad personalization is limited accordingly by Google's own systems.

8 Data Security

Your game progress and settings are stored locally on your device only and are never transmitted to us. The only data leaving your device is the Advertising ID sent to Google AdMob for ad serving, handled under Google's own security and privacy practices.

9 Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time — for example, if we add new ad formats or features. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically. Continued use of the App after any changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
